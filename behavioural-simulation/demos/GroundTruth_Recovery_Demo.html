◆ Ground-Truth Parameter Recovery · interactive validation

A recovered parameter can only be trusted if it can be checked against a known truth, which real investors do not provide. We therefore construct synthetic investors with pre-set behavioural parameters, embed those parameters in years of simulated trading, and test whether the estimator recovers them from behaviour alone. Time-varying psychology

Recovering a time-varying parameter

One investor's loss aversion was configured to rise during the 2022 market crash and relax afterwards (blue). A rolling re-estimation over time (gold) tracks the moving value, which is why the profile is re-estimated continuously rather than fixed once.

Every value shown is recovered from simulated behaviour; no parameter is read directly. The estimator recovers a known hidden input reliably, and the interpretation of that input as a behavioural trait is grounded in prospect and regret theory.
